Biography

Nicholas Korom, also known as Nick Korom, is an entrepreneur and real estate investor whose work focuses on the acquisition of short-term rental properties. He is the founder and Chief Accelerating Officer of BNB Accelerator, which operates as My BnB Accelerator, LLC. The company helps clients source, evaluate and purchase Airbnb and vacation rental investment properties in the United States.[1][2]

His work centers on the decisions and coordination involved in buying a property: selecting a market, reviewing a deal, negotiating terms and moving the transaction through closing. Clients retain ownership of the property and make the purchase decisions.[2]

Career

Earlier businesses and exit

Before his work in short-term rental acquisitions, Korom built businesses in other industries. His personal biography identifies Patient Rhino, LLC as a company he built and sold in 2022, describing the sale as a multi-seven-figure exit.[1] In a public LinkedIn post, Korom likewise states that he sold his first company in 2022 and describes the experience as a turning point in his move toward real estate.[4]

His biography dates his first property investments to 2019 and describes a subsequent shift toward Airbnb and vacation rental properties. That background in business ownership and property investing preceded his focus on coordinating acquisitions for other investors through BNB Accelerator.[1]

BNB Accelerator

BNB Accelerator provides a done-for-you short-term rental acquisition service. Its work spans property sourcing, underwriting, negotiation and closing coordination. The company also helps arrange the transition from purchase to launch, including introductions to furnishing and local operating partners.[2]

The acquisition service is distinct from ongoing property management. Local managers or co-hosts handle day-to-day operations after the acquisition and setup process; BNB Accelerator helps clients connect with those providers.[3]

Acquisition process

The company's published process describes a sequence that brings the property, its financial assumptions and its operating requirements into the same purchase review.[2]

  • Market selection: reviewing demand, seasonality, local rental rules and available inventory.
  • Deal sourcing: identifying potential properties and screening them against the buyer's goals.
  • Underwriting: evaluating rental revenue assumptions, comparable properties, financing, operating expenses and reserves.
  • Due diligence: checking property condition, zoning, permits and association restrictions before purchase.
  • Negotiation and closing: coordinating purchase terms and the steps required to complete the transaction.
  • Launch handoff: arranging furnishing, photography, listing preparation and introductions to local management providers.

Areas of focus

Korom's professional focus includes short-term rental property sourcing, acquisition coordination, deal evaluation and portfolio expansion. Through BNB Accelerator, these activities are connected to the practical work of preparing an acquired property for guests.[1][2]
